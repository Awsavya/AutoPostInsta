# Voiceover script: read these 6 lines

Record on your phone in a quiet room (a closet or under a blanket works great), phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
Talk like you're telling a friend, not like an announcer. One file with all six lines is fine: leave a 2-second pause
between lines. Say AWSAVYA your way: that recording becomes the pronunciation.

Save the file into: `video/painted-ad/assets/recordings/` (any format: m4a, mp3, wav, ogg).

| # | Line | Must fit in |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No time to post on Instagram? | ~2.5 s |
| 2 | And the orders keep piling up. | ~2.3 s |
| 3 | Meet your helper, built by AWSAVYA. It makes your ad for you, | ~3.5 s |
| 4 | and posts it on time, every single day. | ~3 s |
| 5 | You bake. It posts. | ~2 s |
| 6 | Your Instagram, on autopilot. AWSAVYA dot in. | ~3 s |

Tips: smile while you talk (it can be heard), keep lines 1, 4 and 6 brisk, and don't worry about small mistakes:
I'll cut, clean, level and time everything to the video.
