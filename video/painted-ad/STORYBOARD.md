# AWSAVYA auto-posting: painted reel storyboard

Style: hand-painted watercolour and ink cartoon (p5.brush), boiling linework, paper grain, flat 2D, camera always moving,
something happens in every shot, every seam a story transition. Vertical 1080 x 1920, ~19 s, 100 BPM, own synthesized
music + sound effects. Text only twice: one hand-lettered hook, and the end card.

Logline: Riya the baker wants to show today's croissants on Instagram, but she's buried in dough, so a little helper
(the auto-posting app AWSAVYA builds) makes and posts her ad every morning while she bakes.

World: one warm bakery at dawn. Palette: cream paper, ochre and crust gold, terracotta, flour white, night indigo
in the window turning to morning gold as the story lifts. Brand yellow #FFC83D appears only on the helper.

Cast:
- Riya: small cartoon baker, round head, bun of hair, apron, flour on her cheeks, dot eyes. Acts big.
- Tile: AWSAVYA's helper, a cream square like a photo tile with a yellow corner, two dot eyes, stubby legs,
  a lens-dot on its front. Cheerful, quick, a little proud. (Our own character; Clawd belongs to Anthropic.)
- The phone: a character too: it buzzes, hops, sulks with an empty grid, then fills with posts and hearts.

Motif: the phone's empty grid (shot A) becomes a full, glowing grid with hearts (shot E).

Riya's arc: frazzled -> overwhelmed -> surprised -> delighted -> relaxed.

Shots:
  A  0.0-3.2   [in: iris opens from the oven glow]  Dawn. Riya kneads furiously, flour puffs on every beat.
               The phone on the counter buzzes and hops; its screen is an empty grid. Riya glances, sweat drop, back to dough.
               Hand-lettered hook paints on above: "NO TIME TO POST?"  Camera: slow push in.
  B  3.2-6.4   [whip pan with smear]  The rush: trays slide in and pile up on the beat, the wall clock spins,
               the phone is buried under croissants and gives a muffled buzz. Riya's eyes swirl.  Camera: shakes on hits.
  C  6.4-10.0  [match cut: the oven door swings open, light floods out]  Out hops Tile, landing with a squash.
               It salutes, lifts its lens, FLASH (glow) on the croissants, then paints an ad in the air with three
               quick brush strokes: a square, a croissant, a yellow label. A check mark pops.  Camera: pushes to Tile.
  D  10.0-13.4 [carry: the painted ad flies into the dug-out phone]  The phone's grid fills, one post per day:
               the window outside flips night/day four times; each new post lands with a pop. Hearts float up.
  E  13.4-16.0 [camera pulls back]  Calm morning bakery. Riya takes a bite of a croissant, delighted; Tile rides on
               her shoulder; the phone hums happily with hearts. Rhyme with A: same counter, same push, now calm.
  F  16.0-19.0 [out: brush wipe in brand yellow]  Painted end card: AWSAVYA logo, "Your Instagram, on autopilot.",
               yellow button "TALK TO US · AWSAVYA.IN". Tile pops up beside the button and waves. Hold 2.5 s.

Sound: soft dawn pad and a ticking clock (A), the rush builds with kick and hits on each tray (B), a one-beat break
as the oven opens, then the groove drops with Tile (C), pops per post and a bright melody (D), relaxed outro (E), resolve (F).
